# P&W Sable Fix 1.5.3 — Pantograph arcs and animation timing

## English

- Added CEE-style spark, smoke and electric particles to P&W pantographs, including collectors on moving Sable structures.
- Contact and separation effects now follow the actual animated collector head instead of a fixed delay. The spark and the selected short-contact / long-departure arc recording start together.
- Added contact latching to prevent the raising animation's bounce, wire seams and brief electrical telemetry changes from replaying the effect.
- Electrical contact and supply state remain server-authoritative. An overhead wire and an energized contact are required; raising without a wire does not create an arc.
- Advances the normal GeckoLib animation controller offscreen as well, so looking away does not suppress contact audio.
- Added CEE-configured high-speed, rain and cold-weather sparking while drawing current.

Use `pwsablefix-1.5.3.jar` on both client and server. CEE remains `1.21.1-1.1.4`; the other required mod versions are unchanged.

## 中文

- 为 P&W 受电弓加入 CEE 风格的火花、烟雾及电弧粒子，兼容 Sable 移动物理结构。
- 接触与脱离效果改为根据实际动画弓头判断，取消固定延时；粒子与之前选择的“接触短电弧、脱离长电弧”同时触发。
- 升弓回弹、线段交界及短暂的电气读数变化不再反复触发接触效果。
- 接触网位置和带电状态仍由服务端确认；没有实际导线或没有带电接触时，不会凭空产生接触电弧。
- 视野外仍推进原有 GeckoLib 动画，转开镜头也能听到正常的接触音效。
- 带负载运行时，按 CEE 的高速、雨天、寒冷环境配置产生偶发火花。

客户端和服务端同时替换为 `pwsablefix-1.5.3.jar`。CEE 继续使用 `1.21.1-1.1.4`，其他前置版本不变。
